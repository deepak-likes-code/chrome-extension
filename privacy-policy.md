# Privacy Policy for Floez – Productive New Tab

Last updated: [Current Date]

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Floez ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Chrome extension.

## Information We Collect

Our extension collects:

- Browsing history (only for blocked sites functionality)
- To-do list items
- Timer settings
- User preferences
- Site blocking preferences

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API.

## How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely to:

- Provide the extension's core functionality
- Improve user experience
- Track blocked site attempts
- Manage to-do lists and timer settings

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored locally on your device
- We do not transmit any personal data to external servers
- Your data remains private and under your control

## Third-Party Services

Our extension does not share data with any third-party services.

## User Rights

You can:

- Clear all stored data through Chrome's extension settings
- Uninstall the extension to remove all stored data
- Access and modify your data through the extension interface

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes through the extension or Chrome Web Store listing.
